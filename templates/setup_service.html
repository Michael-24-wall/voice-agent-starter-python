Your agent answers the call, collects the caller's name, callback number, problem, and how soon they need help, then sends you a summary you can act on from the call log.

  2. The agent takes the details and reads them back.
  3. A summary lands in your call history on the dashboard.
  4. You call the caller back from the number you saved.
